## Day one

Collect your badge at reception on the ground floor, then find your desk on the third floor. Your laptop is on it, already enrolled in device management; the temporary password is in the sealed envelope underneath, and you will be asked to replace it with a passkey the first time you sign in.

Your buddy for the first fortnight is named in the welcome email. They have blocked their mornings for you, so use them: where the kitchen is, which meeting rooms can be booked without asking, how the coffee machine works. By the end of the day you should have signed in to email, chat and the service catalogue, and have had lunch with your team.

## The first week

Monday is for your own team: what it does, who it serves and what it is working on this quarter. On Tuesday you meet the people you will work with across teams, usually over two or three short calls your manager has arranged. Wednesday is for the customers your team serves, through recorded calls and the account notes in the customer system.

Thursday and Friday are yours. Finish setting up your workstation, complete the information security basics, and read the employee handbook and the customer support playbook. On Friday afternoon your manager will ask what surprised you; there is no wrong answer, and what you say helps the next person who joins.

## Who to ask

Anything about pay, leave or equipment goes to People, through the request form in the service catalogue. Anything about a system goes to the team that runs it, which the catalogue also names. A question about a customer goes to that customer's account manager.

When in doubt, ask your buddy. That is what they are for, and nobody expects you to know where anything is in your first month.
