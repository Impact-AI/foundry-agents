# Code of conduct

Everyone taking part in this project — in issues, pull requests, reviews and discussions — is expected to be considerate, respectful and constructive. Disagree with ideas, not people; assume good faith; give others the benefit of the doubt a reader cannot see your tone.

Harassment, personal attacks, discriminatory remarks and the publishing of others' private information are not accepted. Report a problem to the maintainers through the repository's private channels, and it will be looked at promptly and in confidence. A maintainer may edit, remove or decline any contribution that does not meet this standard, and may exclude a participant who keeps breaking it.

This text follows the [Contributor Covenant](https://www.contributor-covenant.org), version 2.1, in spirit; read it for the full statement of standards and enforcement.
