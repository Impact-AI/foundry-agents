title: Depot opening and closing checklist
summary: What the shift lead checks when a depot opens and closes, so every van leaves loaded and every parcel is accounted for.

## Opening

The shift lead arrives thirty minutes before the first driver. Switch off the alarm, walk the loading bay and check that both roller doors open and close fully. Read the overnight notes from the closing lead: a van left in for repair, a delivery that came back, a door that stuck.

Then check the vans. Each one needs its tablet charged and signed in, its fuel or charge above half, and its tyres and lights checked by the driver before the keys are handed over. A van that fails a check stays in the yard and is reported in the fleet system before the shift starts, not at the end of it. Finally, print nothing: the routes are on the tablets, and the paper run sheets are kept only as a fallback for a tablet that fails.

## During the shift

Keep the loading bay clear of anything that is not being loaded in the next hour. A parcel without a label goes to the exceptions shelf, never into a van, and the exceptions list is checked at noon and again at three.

When a driver calls in a problem, such as a closed address, a damaged parcel or a customer who refuses delivery, the shift lead decides what happens and records it on the delivery itself, so that the customer's account manager sees the same story the driver told. Breaks are staggered so that somebody who can sign for an incoming delivery is always on the floor, and the shift lead's own break is covered by a named colleague.

## Closing

Every van is back, plugged in and locked, and every tablet is docked to charge. Count the parcels that came back against the list the tablets report; a difference is investigated before anybody goes home. Write the overnight notes for the opening lead, set the alarm, and check both roller doors from outside.
